\section{Fundamental Group and Covering Theory}

Homotopy groups are a class of homotopy invariant functors, and can be understood as counting ``spherical holes'' in (pointed) spaces. These functors are of the form \[
\pi_n \coloneq [(S^n,e_1),\cdot]_* : \hToppt \to \Set
\]
which are represented by $n$-dimensional (pointed) spheres.

The fundamental group is the case of the one-dimensional sphere $S^1$.
